# Melodic Voice

Melodic Voice is my capstone project for the FlyRank AI Internship.

## Overview

Melodic Voice is an AI-powered speech development platform designed to help children aged **3–12** who face speech difficulties. The platform combines personalized AI analysis, therapist collaboration, and engaging learning experiences to support children in improving their speech at home and during therapy.

Parents can securely upload their child's speech recordings, choose their preferred language, and connect with qualified speech therapists. Melodic Voice analyzes speech patterns, tracks progress over time, and recommends personalized exercises based on each child's needs.

## Key Features

- AI speech analysis that tracks a child's progress over time.
- Personalized stories and songs that repeatedly practice difficult words.
- Offline-compatible headset with recording and playback controls.
- Parent-controlled privacy for sharing recordings with authorized professionals.
- Therapist dashboard for assigning exercises and monitoring progress.
- Search and connect with qualified speech therapists through ratings and reviews.
- Downloadable learning content for offline practice.
- Resources for parents and teachers to support speech development.

Melodic Voice aims to make speech support more engaging, accessible, and personalized by combining AI technology with expert guidance, helping children build confidence through interactive learning experiences.
